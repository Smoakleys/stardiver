Stardiver · playable loop prototype · v1

Feel the core loop

A playable sketch of the whole loop on the approved star map. Tap Kestrel IV, send an army, fire orbital support while the battle runs, then open your base to spend the rewards. Time runs about 60x fast so you can feel a full day in minutes. The base screen is a functional placeholder (its art isn't designed yet), and every name and number is a placeholder from the design doc.

Turn your phone sideways for the best view.

What to judge

  • Does sending an army and calling strikes feel like your part of a bigger war?
  • Is there always something to do, or did you ever sit waiting?
  • Do the rewards land often enough: toasts, Power ticking up, the operation bar moving?
  • Is the droid chatter the right dose of funny?
  • What felt slow, confusing, or flat?
